CYBERSPACE

Find the network inconsistency. Destroy it before it destroys you.

Ten devices float in this sector, wired together by BGP sessions, VRFs and static routes. Most links are healthy — a calm green laser joins two devices whose configuration actually agrees.

But a few interfaces reference a peer that doesn't exist: a ghost BGP neighbor, a VRF mismatch, a neighbor address outside its own subnet, a dead OSPF adjacency. Those fields glow red and arm a sweeping laser turret. Touch the beam and you lose a life.

Fly in, dodge the sweep, and shoot the red emitter to fix the inconsistency. Clear all of them to win.

  • A/D or ←/→ — rotate
  • W or ↑ — thrust
  • SPACE / left click — fire
  • click a device — inspect its configuration
  • P / ESC — pause
